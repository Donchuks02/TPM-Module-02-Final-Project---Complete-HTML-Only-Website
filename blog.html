Pastor's Weekly Reflection

Published November 14, 2026

“Be still, and know that I am God.”

Psalm 46:10 (King James Version)

In the busyness of each week, taking time to be still helps us remember that God is present and faithful. Quiet moments of prayer can renew our trust and give us strength for the days ahead.

As you go through this week, make room to listen, give thanks, and show kindness to those around you. May your faith bring you peace and encourage others.
